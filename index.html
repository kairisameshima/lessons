Security · Interactive

The Envelope Bank

Envelope encryption as a miniature bank. A teller locks your letter with a one-time data key, a vault keeps the master key that seals it, and the deposit boxes only ever hold locked boxes. A second act covers delegation grants: A has the bank sign a short-lived slip so B can fetch A's access token without ever holding A's keys.

The Checkpoint

How an auth gateway validates north/south traffic from users and east/west traffic between services, told as a border crossing. Travelers sign in with PKCE and get a short-lived entry pass; services trade for transit passes signed with a separate key, naming who they act for and the one service they may call.
